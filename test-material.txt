Intro to Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which plants, algae, and some bacteria convert light energy into chemical energy stored in sugars.
It occurs mainly in chloroplasts, using chlorophyll to absorb light.
The process has two main stages: the light-dependent reactions and the Calvin cycle.

Light-dependent reactions capture energy from sunlight to produce ATP and NADPH, and they split water to release oxygen.
The Calvin cycle uses ATP and NADPH to fix carbon dioxide into glucose and other carbohydrates.

Key factors affecting photosynthesis include light intensity, carbon dioxide concentration, and temperature.
